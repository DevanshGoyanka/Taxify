                            List of Production URLs for Type 2 – ERIs



Please find below the List of Production- URLs for the APIs currently available for Type 2 ERI Users. Please
note       before       you         attempt     to      Hit       these       APIs,        ensure       that:
1) IP addresses are whitelisted at our end.

2) Public-key certificates are successfully updated at our end. ( All Certificate validations must be
completed.)

3)Production      API       credentials     are      configured      (Client-ID       and         Client-Secret)

For the payload details for the below APIs can be downloaded from the ITD portal, Link for your
reference:https://www.incometax.gov.in/iec/foportal/api-specifications

For any further queries please contact erihelp@incometax.gov.in, highlighting the relevant Environment
(UAT/Prod) in the subject line.
