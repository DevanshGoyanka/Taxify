                                 List of UAT URLs for Type 2 – ERIs



      Please find below the List of UAT- URLs for the APIs currently available for Type 2 ERI Users
      for Integration and Testing. Please note before you attempt to Hit these APIs, ensure that:
      1) IP address, Email & Mobiles are whitelisted at our end.

      2) Public-key certificates are successfully updated at our end. ( All Certificate validations must be
      completed.)

      3)UAT      API       credentials     are     configured      (Client-ID      and      Client-Secret)

      For the payload details for the below APIs can be downloaded from the ITD portal, Link for your
      reference:https://www.incometax.gov.in/iec/foportal/api-specifications

      For any further queries please contact erihelp@incometax.gov.in, highlighting the relevant
      Environment (UAT/Prod) in the subject line.
